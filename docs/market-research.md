## Takeaways applied to Gulzor

- **Price bands** in the demo catalog: small bouquets ~140–260k, typical 250–550k, premium 550k–1.45M so'm.
  The homepage "by budget" bands (≤250k, 250–500k, 500k–1M, >1M) match these clusters.
- **Per-stem ordering** is common (tulips and roses priced per stem), so products support "choose the number of stems",
  with odd counts by default, a local gifting convention.
- **Delivery**: a flat fee per zone (~30 000 so'm in the one shop that published it), minimum orders, and same-day delivery are
  standard. Gulzor makes fee, free threshold, minimum order, cut-off and lead time configurable per zone and shows the fee before checkout.
- **Telegram** is the dominant ordering/contact channel. Hence the owner notifications and a customer Telegram link.
- **Occasions** that drive demand: 8 March, Teachers' Day (1 Oct), birthdays, weddings, Valentine's Day.
- **Seasonality**: peonies are seasonal (modelled with seasonal months); tulip demand peaks in spring.

None of these figures are wholesale costs or guaranteed current prices. The owner must set real prices in the admin panel.
